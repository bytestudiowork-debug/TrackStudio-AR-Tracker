# Camera and sensor data

Device-provided camera and motion-sensor metadata can vary by manufacturer, device model and hardware capability. Some values may be unavailable or may describe device estimates rather than measured calibration.

Recorded sensor data and diagnostic values should not be treated as calibrated world-space measurements. Structural checks alone do not establish physical accuracy.

Capture files may contain camera imagery, device metadata and motion-sensor information. Keep exported recordings private and share them only with people you trust.
